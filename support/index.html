常見問題

怎麼把字換成英文？
閱讀時在任何一個字上點兩下，它就會在句子裡變成英文；按右上角「EN」可整章用英文閱讀，這時點兩下會換回中文。

怎麼收藏單字？
長按一個字打開單字卡，按「收藏」。到「單字」分頁開始複習。

需要網路嗎？
不需要，所有書籍都內建在 App 裡。

FAQ

How do I swap a word into English?
Double-tap any word while reading. Tap "EN" at the top right to read the whole chapter in English; double-tap then swaps a word back to Chinese.

How do I save a word?
Long-press a word to open its card and tap "Save word". Review on the Vocabulary tab.

Do I need internet?
No, all books are built into the app.
