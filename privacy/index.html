隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

適用 App：StoryFlip（iOS）　最後更新：2026 年 11 月 15 日

簡單說

  • 不需要帳號，我們不收集姓名、Email、聯絡人、位置等任何可識別你的個人資料。
  • 書籍、翻譯和字庫都內建在 App 裡，可離線使用；閱讀進度、書籤和收藏的單字只存在你的手機上。
  • 不追蹤你、不跨 App 或網站追蹤，也不把資料賣給任何人。
  • 可以在 App 的「我的 → 分享匿名使用數據」隨時關閉匿名使用統計。

存在你手機裡的資料

閱讀進度、書籤、書架、收藏的單字與複習紀錄、閱讀設定。刪除 App 時會一起刪除。

匿名使用數據與閃退回報（可關閉）

為了找出閃退、了解哪些功能好用，App 會透過 PostHog 傳送匿名的使用事件，例如開啟或讀完哪一章、查詢或收藏單字、跟讀分數、是否打開訂閱頁，以及閃退時的錯誤資訊和裝置型號、系統版本。這些資料不含帳號、姓名或 Email，不建立個人檔案、不錄製畫面，也不用 IP 推算位置。關閉「分享匿名使用數據」後就不再傳送。

訂閱與購買

StoryFlip Pro 透過 Apple App Store 購買，付款由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。訂閱狀態透過 RevenueCat 驗證，RevenueCat 會收到 Apple 提供的購買紀錄（產品、日期、匿名的 App 使用者代碼），用來確認你是否有有效的 Pro 訂閱。

跟讀練習（麥克風與語音辨識）

只有在你按下「開始跟讀」時，App 才會請求麥克風和語音辨識權限。語音由 Apple 的語音辨識處理，支援的 iPhone 會直接在裝置上完成；StoryFlip 不會保存或上傳你的錄音，只會在畫面上比對你唸對了哪些字。

朗讀

朗讀功能使用 iPhone 內建的語音，在裝置上處理。

兒童

本 App 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不收集兒童的個人資料。部分書籍含有懸疑、恐怖或犯罪題材。

政策變更與聯絡

若本政策有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方日期。問題請寄 pericprew@gmail.com。

App: StoryFlip (iOS) · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

  • No account. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or any other information that identifies you.
  • Books, translations and the word list are built into the app and work offline. Reading progress, bookmarks and saved words stay on your iPhone.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell data.
  • You can turn anonymous usage statistics off anytime in Profile → Share anonymous usage data.

Data on your device

Reading progress, bookmarks, shelf, saved words and review history, and reading settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports (optional)

To find crashes and learn which features help, the app sends anonymous usage events through PostHog — for example which chapter was opened or finished, word lookups and saves, read-aloud practice scores, whether the subscription screen was opened — plus error details, device model and OS version when the app crashes. These events contain no account, name or email; no person profiles are created, no screens are recorded and IP addresses are not used to work out location. Turning off "Share anonymous usage data" stops them.

Subscriptions and purchases

StoryFlip Pro is bought through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment details. Subscription status is verified with RevenueCat, which receives the purchase record from Apple (product, dates and an anonymous app user ID) to confirm whether you have an active Pro subscription.

Read-aloud practice (microphone and speech recognition)

The app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access only when you tap "Read aloud". Your voice is processed by Apple's speech recognition — on the device on supported iPhones. StoryFlip does not store or upload recordings; it only compares which words you read correctly on screen.

Read aloud

Read-aloud uses the iPhone's built-in voices on the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no children's information. Some books include suspense, horror or crime themes.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: pericprew@gmail.com.
